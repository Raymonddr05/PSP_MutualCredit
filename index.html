An Open Network Where Moral Good Thrives

Our vision is a world where everyone is safe, free, and treated fairly, and able to grow, belong, and contribute.

We build it step by step, starting with a problem businesses feel today: small businesses run short of cash because other businesses pay them late and banks rarely fill the gap.

Our first tool is debt clearing. If A owes B, B owes C, and C owes A, the three debts cancel and no money moves. In Slovenia, clearing cancelled debts between businesses equal to 7.58% of GDP in 1992. In Italy, real invoices show it would cancel about 25% of what firms owe each other.

Members also get a free debt map, fair dispute settlement by peers they choose, and a record of kept payment commitments that they own and take along if they leave. Honest dealing builds standing that members can see.

It is open source and owned by everyone in it. Anyone who accepts the same frameworks can join, and anyone can leave at any time. Its only income is a small share of the value it clears: no cancelled debt, no fee.

We start in the Philippines and prove each step before the next: a lawful path, businesses willing to share invoices, and real debt cancelled on real invoices. We report the results either way, and businesses join by choice, never by force.

We do not yet know whether this alone reaches the vision, and we will add other mechanisms if the evidence says we need them.

We are looking for payments lawyers, software engineers, finance and supply-chain leaders, trade associations, businesses willing to share anonymized invoices, and funders. If that is you, we offer an opportunity to do your life's work.

Now is the time. Join us.
